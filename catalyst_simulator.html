📈 Practice a real catalyst — how to load a 1-minute CSV

Want to drill a real move instead of the demo? Load a 1-minute CSV of the stock's catalyst day — a few months of history is plenty. The file just needs columns Date/Time, Open, High, Low, Close (Volume optional), then hit ⬆ Load CSV up top and trade it minute by minute.

Free places to grab one:

  • Alpha Vantage — free API key; pull 1-min history month-by-month (TIME_SERIES_INTRADAY, interval=1min, datatype=csv). Best free option for older dates.
  • Polygon.io — free tier includes 1-minute bars (rate-limited, ~2 years back).
  • Stooq — some tickers offer intraday CSV downloads, no account needed.

One rule: the file has to already be 1-minute data — you can roll minutes up into a daily view, but you can't turn a daily file into minutes. Your file stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded or stored.

Trading Terms — Quick Glossary

Everything you need to understand what the buttons and the order book are doing.
Long — Buying a stock expecting it to go up. "Buy low, sell high." You own the shares. Marked B / S on the chart.
Short (Sell Short) — Betting a stock goes DOWN. You sell shares you've borrowed, and profit if the price falls. Here: hit SELL while you're Flat. Marked S/S on the chart.
Cover (Buy to Cover) — Closing a short by buying the shares back. Cover lower than you shorted = profit; higher = loss. Marked S/B on the chart.
Flatten — Close your entire position at once — long or short — back to zero (Flat).
Bid / Ask — The Bid is the highest price buyers will pay; the Ask is the lowest price sellers will take. You buy at the Ask and sell at the Bid.
Spread — The gap between Bid and Ask. Tight spread = liquid, cheap to trade; wide spread = you lose more just entering and exiting.
Level 2 — The order book: all the resting buy and sell orders stacked by price, showing supply, demand, and the spread. (The one on this page is simulated for practice.)
Average Cost — Your blended entry price across every share in your current position.
Unrealized P&L — Paper profit/loss on the position you still hold. It moves with every bar.
Realized P&L — Profit/loss you've locked in by closing out. This is the real score.
Equity — Your total account value: cash plus the current value of your position. The number that actually matters.
