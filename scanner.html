What this is. A once-daily scan that flags stocks where a faster moving average crosses above a slower one — a classic trend-turning signal. It runs after the close across a fixed watchlist of ~90 liquid, active tickers (listed below). The exact moving-average settings are our own.

How to read it. Signals are grouped by freshness: Today is a brand-new cross, 1 Day / 2 Days are recent, and Near Cross means the fast average is closing in from below — an early heads-up. The MA gap shows how far the fast average now sits above the slow one; RSI gives the momentum zone; Vol flags unusual volume vs. the recent average; a news tag shows whether recent headlines skewed positive or negative.

What it is not. Not real-time, not a buy list, not financial advice. A crossover is a starting point for your own research — plenty of them fail (see whipsaw). Want to test how this kind of signal actually performs? Run it in the strategy simulator.
