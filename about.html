Why I Built This

A free, honest path for new traders — built by someone who had to learn the hard way.

I'm an Army veteran, a husband, and a father. Before any of this, I was a printer. I tried college, but it didn't work out — agoraphobia and anxiety had other plans. So the path I took to get here wasn't the straight kind.

I got into trading five years ago, and I started the way a lot of people do: I bought a trading program. It was worth the money — but it wasn't built for beginners. It assumed you already knew things I didn't. I made mistakes and missteps early on, and looking back, most of them came down to one thing: ignorance. Not stupidity — just not having anyone to teach me what I didn't know to ask.

That gap — between where a beginner starts and where they finally become profitable — is exactly what this site exists to close.

Why It's Free

I built Alpha Breakout Lab to make that transition easier for the next person. To give people an honest, truthful, free path to learn how to trade — without the scams, the hype, and the paywalls that got between me and the answers when I was starting out.

There are no picks to sell here. I'm not going to hand you a list of stocks and tell you to trust me. I'd rather teach you to think for yourself, because that's the only thing that actually lasts. Following someone else's picks isn't learning to trade — it's just hoping.

What I Actually Believe

I believe the market can be a vehicle to change financial lives. But it doesn't work for people who only want the money and skip the learning — it just doesn't work that way. Most people aren't interested in learning; they're interested in making money. So I decided to help them do the part they'd rather skip. Because that's the part that actually changes things.

And if I'm honest about what makes this different from most of what's out there, it comes down to something simple: I'm not greedy. The education stays free. Forever. That's the whole point.

The One-Stop Shop I Wish I'd Had

When I started, everything was scattered. The lessons were on one site, the charts on another, the tools cost extra, and half of it was locked behind a paywall or a sales pitch. I spent more time hunting for the right thing than actually learning. If someone had just put it all in one honest place — the education, the simulator, the calculators, the catalyst calendar, the glossary — my trading life would have been so much easier.

So that's what I'm building. One place where a new trader can learn the concepts, test a strategy before risking a dollar, size a position, check for upcoming catalysts, and look up any term they don't know — all free, all in one spot. The one-stop shop I wish I'd had.

One More Thing

I was born with a hole in my heart. I wasn't supposed to live through the first night. I did. After that, they said I'd be a vegetable until I was seven, and then I'd die. I lived.

I've spent my whole life doing things I wasn't supposed to be able to do. Building this — teaching people something real, for free, the way I wish someone had done for me — is just the latest one.
